📋 Corrigo Cheat Sheet

SIMS Construction — For Your Phone

🔐 Open Corrigo Login

Getting In

1. Open Safari on your phone (NOT the Corrigo app)
2. Go to am-desktop.corrigopro.com
3. Type dad's email and password
4. Tap LOGIN — no text code, no loop
5. Tap the CorrigoPro tile on the home page

Finding Work Orders

1. You're on the CruChat page — all work orders live here
2. Tap the WO State dropdown at the top
3. Select Waiting for Acceptance — these are new jobs
4. New jobs are in yellow boxes
5. Tap a yellow box to open it

Reading a Work Order

Scroll through to find:
• MESSAGE (orange text) — tap for customer's notes
• Site contact — who to talk to
• Service location — the address
• The issue — what needs fixing
• SLA — deadline
• NTE — max they'll pay without a quote
Tip: Screenshot this and read it to dad on the phone.

Accepting or Rejecting

1. Read the job to dad over the phone
2. Dad says "take it" or "pass"
3. Tap ACCEPT (green) or REJECT (red) at top
4. Done — Corrigo timestamps it automatically

Bidding (Jobs Over $500)

1. Find: "You cannot invoice us more than $___ for this work. SUBMIT QUOTE if you need this increased"
2. Tap SUBMIT QUOTE
3. Two ways to fill it:
• Line items: Tap "Please Select" → pick from price list → enter Description, QTY, Rate, Tax
• Total amount: Enter total dollar amount + description (250 char limit)
4. Tap SUBMIT
5. Church approves or rejects — you'll see it in the CruChat
6. Tell dad whether it was approved
How to figure the bid: Ask dad. He knows his costs. You just type what he says.

Checking In (When Dad Starts)

1. Open the work order
2. Tap Check In
3. That's it — start time recorded
Tip: Dad doesn't need to be on-site. You can check him in from your phone before he leaves.

Checking Out (When Dad Finishes)

1. Dad calls: "Done. 4 hours, 4 guys, $400 materials. Paint and sheetrock."
2. Open the work order
3. Tap Check Out
4. A "Complete Work Order" window opens
5. Enter:
• Comment — what dad did (type or paste from app)
• Repair Category — pick from dropdown
• Repair Code — pick from dropdown
6. Tap COMPLETE

Uploading Photos

1. In the work order (CruChat), tap the paperclip/attachment button
2. Your photo gallery opens
3. Pick the photo dad sent you
4. It uploads into the work order
Note: Drag and drop doesn't work on phone — use the attachment button.

Submitting the Invoice

1. Work order must show Completed status first
2. Go to home page → tap Invoices box (or open from CruChat)
3. Enter Invoice date and Invoice number (use WO# or any number)
4. Tap Please Select under Category/Item to add line items
5. Add each item (labor, materials, etc.)
6. Add Tax if applicable
7. Tap Submit
Payment: The church pays dad directly. Corrigo doesn't handle the money.

Zooming In (Text Too Small?)

• Pinch outward with two fingers to zoom in
• Double-tap to zoom on a section
• Drag with one finger to scroll around when zoomed
• Pinch inward to zoom back out
